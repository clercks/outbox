How to Apply

Want to join UICT? Follow the simple steps below to start your application.

Application Steps

  1. Choose the programme you want to study.
  2. Collect the application form from the admissions office.
  3. Fill in the form and attach the required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee and keep your receipt.
  5. Submit your form and wait for the admission letter.

Documents You Need

  • Copy of your academic certificates
  • Copy of your national ID or birth certificate
  • Two passport size photos
  • Application fee payment receipt

Need help?

If you are stuck anywhere, visit the admissions office or open the Contact page to reach us.
